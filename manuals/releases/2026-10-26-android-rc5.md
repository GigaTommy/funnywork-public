# FunnyWork Android 0.1.0-rc.5

October 26, 2026. Android prerelease, build 15. Available from the website. Changes from 0.1.0-rc.4:

- Search directly from the sidebar, with fewer duplicate controls. Pull down to refresh the list.
- A green dot identifies an online computer. Offline, failed and waiting states are easier to distinguish, with a retry option when refreshing fails.
- Distinct icons and colors make running, waiting, completed and failed tasks easier to follow.
- Improved text, controls and layout in dark mode and at larger font sizes.

Download from the official page and install over the existing app to retain your account and saved records. This remains a prerelease. Mac update channels, Windows availability and the iOS TestFlight invitation are unchanged.
